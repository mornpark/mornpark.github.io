Gear & rental prices (checked Oct 27)

Paddleboards (SUP): Boss Frog's (Poipu & Kapa'a) $35/day per board, about $140 for 4–7 days — so two boards run $70/day or roughly $280 for the week. Hoku at Poipu books online; Easy SUP in Kalaheo rents motorized boards (paddle or let the motor work) — call (808) 431-1146 for their rates.

Snorkel gear: Nukumoi Surf Co, right across from Poipu Beach — full set $10/day or $30/week; fins or mask only $8/day; bodyboard $10/day; life jacket $10/day.

Rule of thumb: mornings are the calm window everywhere — trades pick up toward midday and the paddle back gets hard.
